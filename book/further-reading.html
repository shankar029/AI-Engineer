Public research to explore after the worked lessons

Further reading

These papers provide deeper research context for selected topics. They are not beginner substitutes for the worked chapters, and their experiments are not measurements performed by this book. Read the method and assumptions before comparing reported results.

Tokenization

  • Rico Sennrich, Barry Haddow and Alexandra Birch. Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units. ACL, 2016. A research connection for subword BPE.
  • Taku Kudo. Subword Regularization: Improving Neural Network Translation Models with Multiple Subword Candidates. ACL, 2018. Introduces a Unigram-based segmentation approach and probabilistic subword sampling.

Transformers, positions and attention execution

  • Ashish Vaswani and colleagues. Attention Is All You Need, 2017. The original encoder-decoder Transformer; distinguish it from the decoder-only examples here.
  • Jianlin Su and colleagues. RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding, 2021. The rotary-position research connection.
  • Tri Dao and colleagues. FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness, 2022. Studies tiled attention and memory movement beyond the running-sum examples.

A useful way to read a paper

Write down the problem, the input/output shapes, what changes in the algorithm, and what the experiment actually measures. Keep mathematical equivalence, model quality and hardware performance as separate questions. A result on one task or device is not a universal guarantee.
